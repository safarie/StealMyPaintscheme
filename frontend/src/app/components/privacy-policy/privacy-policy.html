Steal My Paint Scheme ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains which personal data we process when you use this website, why we do so, and which rights you have under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR, in Dutch: AVG) and the Dutch GDPR Implementation Act (UAVG).

1. Who is responsible?

The controller for the personal data described in this policy is [your name], the maker of Steal My Paint Scheme, located in the Netherlands. For privacy questions or requests you can contact us at [your contact e-mail].

2. Which data do we collect?

  • Account data: username, e-mail address and password. Your password is stored only as a secure (bcrypt) hash; we can never see it.
  • Content you create: paint schemes, painting steps, your paint inventory and images you upload. Photos can contain hidden metadata (such as the location where the photo was taken). Please remove this before uploading if you do not want to share it.
  • Read confirmation: the date and time at which you confirmed that you have read this privacy policy.
  • Technical data: after logging in, a login token is stored in your browser's local storage so you stay logged in. This is strictly necessary for the website to work, so no permission is required for it (art. 11.7a Dutch Telecommunications Act). We do not use tracking, analytics or advertising cookies.

You need to provide a username, e-mail address and password to create an account. Without them we cannot offer you an account. All other content is optional.

3. Why do we use this data, and on what legal basis?

  • To create and manage your account and let you log in (necessary for the performance of our agreement with you, art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • To store and display your paint schemes and inventory (performance of the agreement, art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • To be able to show that we informed you about this policy (our legitimate interest in demonstrating compliance with the GDPR, art. 5(2) and 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • To keep the website secure, for example by preventing misuse and fixing errors (our legitimate interest in a safe and working website, art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising or profiling. We do not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing (art. 22 GDPR).

4. Who can see your data?

Your e-mail address and password are never shown to other users. Your username and paint schemes may be visible to other visitors of the website, for example in the "All Schemes" overview. Other users can also save a copy of a public paint scheme ("steal" it); such copies belong to that user.

We do not sell or give your data to third parties. The website is hosted by a hosting provider that stores the data on our behalf. This provider acts as our processor and may only use the data on our instructions, under a data processing agreement (art. 28 GDPR). We will name this provider here once the website goes live. Your data is stored within the European Union / European Economic Area; if this ever changes, we will only do so with appropriate safeguards (chapter V GDPR) and update this policy. We only share data with others when we are legally required to do so.

5. How long do we keep your data?

  • Account data, paint schemes, inventory, uploaded images and the read confirmation: as long as you have an account.
  • When you delete your account, this data is removed immediately. Copies of your schemes that other users saved remain part of their account.
  • Technical server logs used for security and error handling are kept for no longer than necessary for that purpose.
  • The login token in your browser is removed when you log out.

6. Security

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data, such as password hashing, access control on personal data and validation of uploaded files.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you (art. 15);
  • have incorrect data corrected (art. 16);
  • have your data deleted, the "right to be forgotten" (art. 17);
  • restrict the processing of your data (art. 18);
  • receive your data in a portable format (art. 20);
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interest (art. 21).

When you are logged in, you can view your account data, change your username and delete your account yourself on the Account page. For all other requests, contact us at the address in section 1. We respond within one month; for complex requests this period can be extended by two more months, in which case we will let you know (art. 12(3) GDPR). We may ask you to confirm your identity first.

If you are not satisfied with how we handle your data, you have the right to file a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

8. Minimum age

This website is intended for people aged 16 and older. If you are younger than 16, please do not create an account.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it was last changed. In case of important changes we will inform you.
